# Belfry Software

> Belfry is the all-in-one Security Guard Management System (SGMS) built for security companies of all sizes. It unifies scheduling, GPS timekeeping, field operations, payroll & HR, and billing into a single platform — eliminating spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and manual back-office work.

Belfry is headquartered at 1239 Broadway, Suite 703B, New York, NY 10001. It raised a $12M Series A to accelerate growth. The platform is used by hundreds of security businesses across the United States and is available on Web, iOS, and Android.

## Product

- [Home](https://www.belfrysoftware.com/): Platform overview, key metrics (+8 hrs saved/week on payroll and scheduling, -60% unbilled OT), customer testimonials, FAQs, and comparison vs. alternatives.
- [Scheduling](https://www.belfrysoftware.com/scheduling): Smart shift matching, fill shifts fast, overtime cost controls, automated schedule updates and notifications.
- [Timekeeping](https://www.belfrysoftware.com/timekeeping): Electronic timesheets auto-submitted at shift end, GPS-synced approvals, officer overlap and OT flagging, automated break alerts and compliance.
- [Operations](https://www.belfrysoftware.com/operations): Real-time client portals, live officer activity via shift drilldown, NFC/geofence checkpoint patrol tours, custom patrol workflows, fast dispatch.
- [Payroll & HR](https://www.belfrysoftware.com/payroll-hr): Payroll synced directly to timesheets, custom pay rates and deductions, next-day direct deposit, ACA compliance, IRS 1094/1095-C e-filing, benefits management, tax compliance across all 50 states.
- [Billing](https://www.belfrysoftware.com/billing): Automated invoice distribution, ACH and credit card payment processing, OT billing and rate multiplier adjustments, profitability reports and dashboards.
